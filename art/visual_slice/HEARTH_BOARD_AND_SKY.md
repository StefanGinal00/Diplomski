# Hearth noticeboard and upper sky — 2026-09-27

Only Cinder's EasternSky plate now fades its top 140 world pixels into the
underlying biome background. Alpha uses unshifted plate UVs, so the blend
boundary does not drift with camera parallax. All other settlement plates
default to no fade; original geometry, UV crop and update cadence are retained.

HearthFieldBoard has seven aligned rows with separate ROUTE/TASK/GUARD/RETURN
columns, descriptive status text and secondary color cues. AFTER BOSS,
TASK/GUARD, READY and WON distinguish progression without inventing payouts.
The footer explicitly says that WON means patrol defeated, not reward claimed.
The existing WorkOrders label now holds totals and this legend. Guidance,
NPC stops, conversation wiring, task triggers, rewards and saves are unchanged.

The framed 380x198 board moves 350px east relative to its office root, into
free wall space below the watch gallery. Two small decorative straps join it
to that gallery. Residents remain in their original positions. Table text,
frame and backing no longer intersect any of the 44 walkable rectangles.
It is still a world-space sign, not a modal quest log or mobile UI overhaul.
All labels ignore pointer input; updates are event-driven, with no new timer.

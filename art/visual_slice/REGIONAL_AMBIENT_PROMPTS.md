## Integration

`RegionalAmbientDressing.gd` combines rubble/urns/fungi/vents with smaller grasses or flowers, and attaches hanging details to surveyed walls or the undersides of real platforms. Each room is capped at 14 two-piece ground compositions and eight hanging cutouts. `WorldAmbience.gd` supplies small multi-frequency wind transforms around a fixed root or hanging attachment, shared with the existing 18/8 active-object budget. These are procedural ambient motions using the cutouts, not newly generated multi-frame character animations.
